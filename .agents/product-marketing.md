# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v3
**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** SHUNPO builds the system that runs your company: custom business software, automations and AI for SMEs.
**What it does:** A Lyon team spends five days on site (the Immersion) to measure where time is lost, then builds a custom system brick by brick: it connects the tools the company already uses, automates repetitive tasks and adds AI agents that prepare the work for teams to validate. SHUNPO then maintains and evolves it every month.
**Product category:** Custom business software agency ("logiciel métier sur mesure", automatisation, IA pour PME).
**Product type:** Service (agency), delivered as owned software.
**Business model:** Paid Immersion (from 5,000 € HT, refunded on the first brick), then a fixed price per brick agreed before it starts, then monthly maintenance and evolution. Eligible projects can use the innovation tax credit (CII, 20 % of eligible spend for an SME); SHUNPO is an approved agency.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** French SMEs of 20 to 250 employees. Priority sectors: accounting firms, property management, construction (BTP), agencies.
**Decision-makers:** CEO / managing director, partner of the firm, operations director.
**Primary use case:** Give time back to teams buried in re-typing, searching and chasing.
**Jobs to be done:**

- Stop re-entering the same data in several tools.
- Find any information without digging through mail, WhatsApp, Excel and Drive.
- Keep the company running when one key person is away.
- See problems before they cost money.

**Use cases:**

- Accounting firms: reminders go out on their own; received documents are sorted and attached to the right file.
- Property management: every intervention request is read, classified and followed to the end; the tenant is kept informed.
- Construction: teams fill site reports from the site; hours and materials come back without re-typing.
- Agencies: time, quotes and invoices are linked; project profitability is visible while the project runs.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** The company runs on disconnected tools and manual work: the same data is typed several times, information is scattered, and the business depends on one or two people.
**Why alternatives fall short:**

- ERP or standard SaaS: teams adapt to the tool, only a fraction of features are used, projects take months, and the editor decides functions, price and pace.
- Freelancer: expert on one topic or average on all; the project needs a business profile, a designer and a developer.
- Internal Excel and no-code patches: fragile, owned by one person.

**What it costs them:** Hours lost every week, errors, slow decisions. [NEED: a measured figure from a real Immersion before quoting any number]
**Emotional tension:** "If Sophie is off, nothing moves." Fear of a long, expensive software project that never lands.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Other software agencies and ESN — fall short because they build what is specified rather than observing the field first, and rarely own business outcomes.
**Secondary:** ERP and SaaS (Odoo, HubSpot, Notion, sector software) — fall short because teams must adapt to them and they don't talk to each other; SHUNPO connects them instead of replacing them.
**Indirect:** Hiring an internal developer or a freelancer — falls short because one profile cannot cover business, design and engineering.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Field first: five days of Immersion before any line of code.
- Brick by brick: fixed scope and price per step, first result in weeks.
- Three profiles on every project: a Business Architect, an Experience Architect and an AI Architect, one person responsible for the result.
- Ownership: code, data and documentation are handed over by contract.
- AI where it saves measurable time, not for show.

**How we do it differently:** Observe, build the costliest process first, measure the time saved, then extend.
**Why that's better:** The company gets an asset that fits how it works, instead of a subscription it has to bend to.
**Why customers choose us:** Speed, rigour and advice (testimonials), and the guarantee that the software belongs to them.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Re-typing, lost information, key-person dependency, flying blind.
**Pull:** A system that follows how they work, first result in weeks, owned by them.
**Habit:** "We've always done it in Excel"; the existing editor contract.
**Anxiety:** Cost drift, team adoption, dependency on the agency.

**Brand platform (brand guide, Magenta 2025):** values Transparence, Rigueur, Justice, Humanité, Innovation, Excellence. Archetypes: the Sage (clear, pedagogical, fact-based) and the Hero (engaged against abusive agency practices, protects clients who are misinformed). Origin: the founder unblocked his own product (Helpsy) in days with low-code, which revealed how much time the right tools give back. The name « Shunpo » means « déplacement instantané », from Japanese culture and video games; Japanese rigour and « l'amour du travail bien fait » shape the identity.

**Tone by context:**

- Support: calm, specific, owns the issue.
- Launch: one concrete outcome, no superlatives.
- Errors: say what happened and what to do.
- Sales: the client's numbers first, ours second.
- Social: field stories, before/after.

**Terms we own:** Immersion, brique par brique, le système qui fait tourner votre entreprise.
**Never use (and why):** invented metrics or testimonials (trust is the product); "AI-powered magic" (AI is a tool measured in hours saved); "révolutionner" and exaggerated promises (they contradict the transparency value).
**Positioning note:** the 2025 brand guide positions SHUNPO as a LowCode/NoCode agency; the 2026 site positions it as custom business software, automations and AI for SMEs. This document follows the 2026 positioning; the brand platform (values, voice, visual identity) still applies.
