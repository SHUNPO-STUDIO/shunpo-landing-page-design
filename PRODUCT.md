# Product

SHUNPO, a Lyon agency, builds the system that runs an SME: custom business software, automations and AI, for companies of 20 to 250 employees. Positioning, audience, proof and voice live in `.agents/product-marketing.md`; this file only lists the surfaces.

## Surfaces

| Surface                   | Mode     | Visitor's success                                                        |
| ------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Landing page (`/`)        | Persuade | Understands what SHUNPO changes for their company and books an Immersion |
| Storybook (`packages/ui`) | Read     | Finds the right component and props                                      |

## Design direction

A day rising over misty mountains, white washi paper and a red hanko seal. Direction and references live in `DESIGN.md`; token values live in `packages/ui/src/styles/theme.css`.
